We are delighted to announce that our own Claus Horn has won the Yale AI for Science grant. The application was led by Claus, together with an interdisciplinary team of Yale researchers spanning neurology, immunobiology, genetics, statistics and computer science: **Professor Xianjun Dong** of the Department of Neurology and the Yale Center for RNA Science and Medicine; **Professor Akiko Iwasaki** of the Department of Immunobiology, the Department of Epidemiology of Microbial Diseases and the Department of Dermatology; **Professor Smita Krishnaswamy** of the Department of Genetics and the Department of Computer Science; **Professor Lu Lu** of the Department of Statistics and Data Science; and **Professor Rex Ying** of the Department of Computer Science.

Progress in AI for science depends on combining methodological expertise in machine learning with deep domain knowledge in biology and medicine. This team unites both: experimental and clinical scientists working alongside researchers in statistics, geometric deep learning and biomedical data science. The grant will form the basis for interdisciplinary collaborations for the development of new AI for science applications in biomedicine.

Congratulations to Claus and to the entire team. We look forward to sharing more about the work as it progresses.
